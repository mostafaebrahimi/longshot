Privacy

Longshot works entirely on your machine. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Screenshots are stitched in the editor tab and stay there until you save or copy them. Your settings sync through your own Chrome profile.

The extension can only read a page after you start a capture on it — by clicking the toolbar icon, using a shortcut, or picking it from the right-click menu.
